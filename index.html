Alicia Certified Life Coach, Soul Stryde
Meet your coach

"I am making it part of my mission to help teens and young adults, and people of every age, reconnect with their inner power."

Alicia is a certified life coach, born and raised in Vancouver, BC. Like most people, she has faced her own setbacks, and as a young adult she wished she had a coach to guide her through those years. That experience shapes how she works today.

She helps people reconnect with their inner power so they can make conscious choices about who they want to be in this world. Her clients range from young children learning life skills and self awareness, to adults working on purpose, goals, relationships, and self love, to business professionals.
